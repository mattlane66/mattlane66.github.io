How to play

Simple rules. Interesting choices.

  1. Start with 21 empty cells and two players: X and O.
  2. On your turn, mark one, two, or three consecutive empty cells, anywhere on the board.
  3. Press Enter to confirm your selection and pass the turn.
  4. The player who fills the last empty cell wins.
