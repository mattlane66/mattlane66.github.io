PayPal · Foundational blockchain work
Director, Product Management · 2016–2018

Before crypto reached the wallet.

PayPal cryptoconsumer capability
Strategic continuity, not the same implementation.
2016–2018Secured internal funding and led four product teams building a real-time gross settlement system on Hyperledger, denominated in IMF Special Drawing Rights.
TodayPayPal has brought crypto into the consumer wallet: buying, selling, sending, receiving, and spending supported digital assets.

The work was foundational. Before PayPal exposed crypto to consumers, we were working on the harder infrastructure question underneath it: how digital value could move and settle inside a global payments network. That work helped establish a path from blockchain as internal payments infrastructure to crypto as a consumer capability.

See PayPal crypto today ↗See PayPal crypto for merchants ↗
This is a portfolio snapshot of my 2016–2018 work and its strategic continuity with PayPal's later crypto products—not a claim that the original settlement system is the current consumer implementation.
