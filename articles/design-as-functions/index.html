Design as Functions

The Fit Check Error Model

A solution can pass every test we give it and still fail to produce the change we wanted.

The error may lie in what we observed, the outcome we chose, our assumptions about the world, the requirements we derived, the alternatives we generated, the choice we made, what we built, or how we interpreted the result.

The abstract model

x → f() → y

This is the design claim: a proposed solution will move a situation toward an intended outcome. It does not establish that the change will happen.
