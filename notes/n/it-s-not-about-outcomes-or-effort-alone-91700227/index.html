It’s Not About Outcomes or Effort Alone

Effort that isn’t aligned with outcomes feels wasteful while prioritizing outcomes over effort leads to conservative, slower decision-making—favoring predictability over progress. True effectiveness comes from balancing both in a way that sustains momentum without sacrificing impact.

1. **Lead with Context, Not Just Outcomes** Focus on understanding the current state and the specific factor you’re trying to improve to meet a desired expectation. Outcomes alone don’t tell the full story—context ensures that effort is directed where it matters most.
2. **Maintain Even-Paced Momentum** Sustainable, effective execution happens when teams operate with rhythm and clarity, avoiding extremes of overcommitment or stagnation. Achieve this by:

- Keeping teams small.
- Prioritizing trust over rigid accountability structures.
- Cultivating a generative culture that enables creativity and adaptability.
- Avoiding 100% capacity utilization (allowing for slack/using buffers) leaves room for strategic collaboration and unexpected issues or opportunities that arise.
- De-risking options before making decisions and committing close to when executing on that decision is imminent.
- Using short-horizon time budgets to cap the downside of a bet.
- Trading quantity for quality.
This is how you create the conditions for building the best possible product—not just once, but repeatedly. It’s not just about making great products; it’s about designing the system that enables sustained excellence.
