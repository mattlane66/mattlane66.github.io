Counterintuitively, sometimes—especially in creative problem-solving endeavors—modeling the messiness of reality requires being imprecise or approximate. Imprecise wiggle room often better reflects what’s truly happening than basing conclusions on a precise but false premise.

The image presented here shows how this idea can be applied to understanding the progress of a project using the Move the Needle feature in Basecamp.

But we can apply this logic to almost anything where human systems and innovation are central to the activity.
