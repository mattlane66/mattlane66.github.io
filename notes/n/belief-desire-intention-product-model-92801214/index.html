Belief–Desire–Intention Product Model

  • **Definition**: This model applies when customers (or users) hold **Strong Beliefs**, which function like **preferences that are not easily changed**.
  • **Opportunity**: Because these beliefs are deeply rooted, a product or service that **effectively aligns with them** can achieve especially high engagement and loyalty.
  • **Conclusion**: **Therefore**, a Strong Belief represents a **massive opportunity**—*if* it is thoughtfully **addressed with a design that fits into the desires and intentions of people**.
