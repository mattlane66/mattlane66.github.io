Zeroth Principles Thinking

Most of the time, we meet the world through self-consciousness, habit, convention, ambition, judgment, preconception.

Sometimes attention clears that away.

Often in nature.

You stop thinking about yourself having the experience.

You receive what is there.

I’m tired of treating ideas like math.

Clichés, homilies, formulas: answers arrived at before the looking begins.

Take in the world and pass it through you.

Look closely.

Take it in.

Let it stay.

Do not bring the form with you.
