Computers Like Us

Routine work turns life into repetition, tasks divided so finely that the person gets lost in the process. Movements become mechanical, thoughts shaped by the rhythm of the task, until everything feels like part of the same engine.

Now machines are given the scripts— AI built to mimic the way humans learn, adapt, and respond. It’s still rules, still patterns dressed up as something alive, but the lines blur.

What once made us human now feels shared, split between flesh and circuit, both trying to make sense of the motions.
