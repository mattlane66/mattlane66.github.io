A Persistent Model of Product Intent

A product should be governed by a persistent model of what must exist, why it must exist, how its parts work together, and what must remain true as the code changes.

Three components make that possible:

  1. Fit Check and Reverse Fit Check establish which requirements the design satisfies and why each part belongs.
  2. Structural interaction schematics establish how those parts connect, interact, and behave.
  3. Design invariants establish what agents must preserve when changing the implementation.
