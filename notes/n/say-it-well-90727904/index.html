Say it Well

Every spoken word is a work in progress. A draft in constant flux.

Articulating the clarity of our inner thoughts demands deliberate effort; we must resist the temptation to shortcut the depth of our understanding. Instead, attune yourself to the flow of your thoughts and commit to expressing that inner dialogue, the central idea, with authenticity.

Whether in debate or casual conversation, be prepared for speech to meander through digressions, imprecise turns of phrase, and non-linear progressions where ideas often vanish as swiftly as they appear.

The art of speaking is an entirely different science from the measured craft of writing.
