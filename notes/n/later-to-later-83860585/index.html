Later to Later

*Later* makes a pile at the end.

Make it so there’s no time for *later*. It’s *now* (or in some short time frame from *now*) or not.

This way *later* isn’t an obligation, but a maybe. Preserve optionality.

*Later* needs to mean: not now, not soon, and not for sure.

Important things come back.

Decide on what to do *now* while also creating space to shape what to consider doing *next*. Only commit to do what might be *next* when you are close to the moment of acting on those decisions.
