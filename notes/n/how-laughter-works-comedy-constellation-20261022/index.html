How laughter works.

Different comedians can arrive at similar comic moves by entirely different routes. An ordinary assumption becomes absurd when taken literally. A small irritation grows into a catastrophe. A sentence leads us one way, then turns against itself.

This constellation is a way to explore those relationships—not a ranking of the funniest people, nor a genealogy of who influenced whom.
