Before and Now

The old plans sit on my desk

like fallen leaves.

I made them with such care then,

believing in what I thought I knew.

Strange how we trust

what we used to think,

as if being further away

makes something more true.

Walking this morning

I saw deer tracks in snow—

they don't follow paths

we cleared for them.

Each step knows more

than all our thinking:

the weight of rain,

the lean of wind,

the way things change

when we're not looking.

You can read tomorrow

in any direction.

But your feet know the earth

right now, right here,

telling you things

you couldn't have written down

before you came.

Just take the next step.

That's all you need to know.

The path will tell you

where to go.
