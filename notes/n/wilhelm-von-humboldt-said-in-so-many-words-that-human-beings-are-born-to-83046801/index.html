Wilhelm Von Humboldt said, in so many words, that human beings are born to inquire and create and to create under their initiative. Any work that a person does under external pressure is inhuman. If a person creates something beautiful under external orders, we may admire what the individual does, but we will despise who they are. That’s classical liberal thought. It has always been based on human dignity.
