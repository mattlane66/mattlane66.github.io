2026-04-29 · Judgment & knowledge

Ideology is premature closure, a prison of prefabricated language.

A civilized empiricism means:

You know your concepts are socially shaped.

You know your tribe distorts your perception.

You know evidence does not simply “speak for itself.”

But you still believe reality exists outside your frame and can correct you.
