The Way It Comes

You lean too hard against the wind.

Let go.

Somewhere, it is already waiting,

watching from the tall grass,

breathing easy.

It was never locked away—

just resting,

just waiting for you

to stop pushing so hard.

Find the weight that fits your hands,

the ache that feels like home.
