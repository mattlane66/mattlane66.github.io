2025-01-01 · AI & future work

Labor Market Predictions

Psychomotor work will be hard to displace and likely durable.

Occupations that require high amounts of trust will stick around.

Future knowledge worker titles will include familiar words like *designer* but also new ones like “Arbiter” and “Ombudsperson”. Non-technical roles that guide and evaluate AI-human alignment should fold into a function headed by a Chief AI Ethics Officer (CAIEO). Someone trained as a technologist and philosopher. We cannot write a stable user manual for these AI systems, at least as of now. We’ll need people to oversee the inputs and outputs from outside the work—those who consider the entire context around the models and the products they fold into.
