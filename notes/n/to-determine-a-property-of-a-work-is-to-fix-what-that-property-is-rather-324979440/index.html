To determine a property of a work is to fix what that property is rather than leave it to another process to supply. A person authors a property only if the person determines it. Therefore, when a generative system supplies a property that the person’s choices left undetermined, the person does not author that property. A person authors a generated work only to the extent that the person determines its properties. Transformation preserves authorship wherever it preserves properties the person determined.
