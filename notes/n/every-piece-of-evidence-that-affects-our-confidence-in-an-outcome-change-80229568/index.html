Every piece of evidence that affects our confidence in an outcome changes its probability and should…

Every piece of evidence that affects our confidence in an outcome changes its probability and should influence our actions. Probabilities reflect our ignorance of the world, not the world itself. As new information reduces our ignorance, it alters the probability.
