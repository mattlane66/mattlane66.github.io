Where Power Rests and How Some Norms Form

In Hume’s paradox, it’s puzzling that so many obey so few. Taleb’s “intolerant minority” clarifies it: a rigid, unyielding few can overrule a more flexible majority. Their uncompromising stance sets norms others conform to, revealing how the many often get governed by the few.
