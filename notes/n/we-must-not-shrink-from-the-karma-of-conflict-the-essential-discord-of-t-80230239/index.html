We must not shrink from the karma of conflict, the essential discord of true argument.

We must not shrink from the karma of conflict, the essential discord of true argument. To slash through the thicket of falsehoods, to lay low the towering follies of our time is not a breach of politeness but an act of intellectual hygiene. It is the necessary work to prepare the ground for a society based not on the comforting lie but the uncomfortable truth.
