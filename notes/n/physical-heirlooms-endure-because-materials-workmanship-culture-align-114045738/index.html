Physical heirlooms endure because materials + workmanship + culture align.

Software can match that durability when licensing + spec discipline + community stewardship play the same role that bronze, spruce, or hand‑wired turret boards do in the physical world. The moment a program is designed with those constraints in mind, it’s no longer disposable tech—it’s a candidate family heirloom waiting to happen.
