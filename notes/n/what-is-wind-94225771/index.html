What Is Wind

What is wind?

Wind is the quiet traveler,

Drifting from high to low,

Where warmth and coolness softly meet.

It carries the sun’s uneven touch

A stirring breath, a fleeting whisper.

Each gust is a thousand murmurs,

A dance of unseen forces,

A step in nature’s measured song.

In its passing, the world is rewritten,

Etched in delicate patterns of motions

A quiet trace of the hidden order that guides us all.
