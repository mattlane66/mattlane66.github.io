Language is the original code for hacking reality

The world is composed of description, and meaning is only found in language.

To find the words that map to what we observe is a kind of alchemy.

The goal is not to turn the world into words. The goal is to get your words as close as you can to the materiality of the world you’re noticing.

Writing isn't just a transcription of pre-formed thoughts, but a collaborative process between thought and language. Good writing isn't just about crafting beautiful sentences, but about ensuring they serve genuine communication.

Pay attention to your subjective experiences and learn how to describe them.
