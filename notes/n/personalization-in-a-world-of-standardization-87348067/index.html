Personalization in a World of Standardization

Adaptive learning systems like Large Language Models (LLMs) bridge the gap between professionals and beginners, creating tools that make knowledge accessible and relatable for all. By tailoring content to students' interests, these systems have the power to transform abstract subjects into engaging, meaningful learning experiences.

LLMs can take personalized education to the next level, fostering an asking-style approach to learning. Through customized projects incorporating computational thinking, LLMs empower students to formalize and bring their ideas to life, making education interactive and personalized at scale.
