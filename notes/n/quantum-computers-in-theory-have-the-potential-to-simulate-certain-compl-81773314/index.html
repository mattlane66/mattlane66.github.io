Quantum computers, in theory, have the potential to simulate certain complex systems, particularly those governed by quantum mechanics, much more efficiently than classical computers. While they may help us understand aspects of life and reality at a fundamental level, their current and near-future capabilities are far from simulating all of life or reality itself.

Quantum computing has **potential** for agent-based modeling in areas where:

- Interactions are probabilistic or highly complex.
- Large-scale optimization problems arise.
- Quantum properties of the agents or system are significant.
