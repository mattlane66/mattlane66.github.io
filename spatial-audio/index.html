Beyond the browser

Imagine LCC in a miniature home-theater computer.

A small dedicated device could bring binaural sound over ordinary stereo loudspeakers to a living room—an LCC On/Off switch for movie nights, games, and music, without opening a webpage.

For loudspeakers only—not headphones. Sit centered in front of a narrow stereo pair. Use a room-filling but comfortable volume; these scenes contain sharp transients and powerful bass.
