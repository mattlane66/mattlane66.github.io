About the LCC algorithm ↗
How to listen — and where these sounds come from

For the intended spatial effect, use two closely spaced stereo loudspeakers and sit on their center line. LCC is not designed for headphone listening. Start quietly; the dinosaur has deep impacts and a sudden roar. The 3D scenes use a generic browser HRTF to create front/back and height cues before LCC processing. These cues vary by listener, speaker geometry, room, and browser: no fixed two-speaker system guarantees a literal source behind every listener.

Orbit, Flyby and Helicopter are original synthesized mono sources moved through the browser’s HRTF spatializer. Dinosaur combines original synthesized creature sounds and footsteps with an optional, low-pitched public-domain growl by stilgar (Wikimedia Commons / PDSounds). If that growl cannot load, the complete synthesized dinosaur scene still works. No microphone capture, third-party players, or uploaded files.

LCC by Robert LiKamWa and Matthew Lane with guidance from Ralph Glasgal. The processing is adapted from the public-domain implementation.
