About capture, browser support and listening rights

A webpage cannot directly read Spotify’s embedded audio or change your Mac's system output. Chrome tab capture uses a browser permission prompt; some browsers do not expose tab audio. The same-tab playlist is provided for discovery, not direct capture. The processing feature is intended for sources you are authorized to process: external audio services may prohibit altered playback even when capture is technically possible. Spotify's platform policies restrict applications that alter Spotify content. Audio capture stays local, but permission to modify music is separate from permission to listen.

BlackHole must be installed and configured separately; the website cannot install drivers or change macOS sound routing. The comparison is meaningful only when the input is stereo and your speakers are arranged as described in the LCC documentation. For safety, start with a low speaker volume. A little delay and loudness variation between modes is possible.

Listen on two closely spaced stereo loudspeakers, sitting centered between them. LCC is designed for this geometry, not headphone playback.
