About the LCC algorithm ↗
How to listen — and where these sounds come from

Listen through two closely spaced stereo loudspeakers while seated on their center line. LCC is designed for speakers, not headphones. Keep the speaker volume modest when comparing modes. These tests have different spatial content: a difference in sound does not automatically mean more accurate spatial localization.

Position, Movement and Passing car are original synthesized tests generated in your browser. Forest is “Birdsong mild sunny day” by stephan, a public-domain recording from PDSounds hosted by Wikimedia Commons. Piano is “Emotional Piano” by triangelx, available under CC0 and hosted by Wikimedia Commons. When a recording cannot be loaded, the page clearly says so and falls back to a locally generated demonstration. The site never uploads or records microphone audio.

LCC by Robert LiKamWa and Matthew Lane with guidance from Ralph Glasgal. The processing is adapted from the public-domain implementation.
