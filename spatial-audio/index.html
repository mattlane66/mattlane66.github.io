What is LCC?

Localization Cue Correction is a lightweight stereo crosstalk-cancellation system. On loudspeakers, sound from the left speaker normally reaches both ears, and vice versa. LCC reduces this interference to preserve more of the left/right spatial cues in a recording.

Unlike binaural headphone playback, the intended result depends on speaker placement and where you sit. For the clearest comparison, keep the same volume, track, and listening position as you toggle.

Algorithm by Robert LiKamWa and Matthew Lane, with guidance from Ralph Glasgal. View the open-source implementation. Audio file titles are based on uploaded filenames; origin and usage rights have not been independently verified.
